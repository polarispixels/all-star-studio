# Team Star: Design

Date: 2026-10-04. Ships as **v0.8.0** (MINOR: new page). This is Track 1, the single logo that represents the whole All-Stars mentoring team, entered in the contest due **2026-10-22**. The only contest rule is no Aetna/CVS heart.

## Options (all on house shape B)

| Letter | Name | What it is | How it's made |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Colorful rays | The homepage star: colored rays from the center with stripes and dots | Existing `assets/star.svg`, copied to `assets/team/a-rays.svg` |
| **B** | Pieces of everyone | One symbol per arm: mountain (Becky, top), phoenix wing (Scott), flame (Dixie), magnifying glass (Detective), pink ribbon (Support), around a small shared center. Placeholders join when claimed. | Drawn by an agent, `assets/team/b-pieces.svg` |
| **C** | Puzzle mosaic | About 15 to 25 small interlocking jigsaw pieces in the team's colors, like a quilt: "many pieces, one team" | Generated by code: a jigsaw grid with seeded tab directions, clipped to shape B, `assets/team/c-mosaic.svg` |
| **D** | Five-piece puzzle | Five large pieces, one per arm, each a different color. Each seam (center to inner valley) has one knob, and every knob points the same way around, giving exact five-fold rotational symmetry like a pinwheel | Generated by code, `assets/team/d-five.svg` |

Generated options come from `tools/build-team.mjs` using `js/star-shape.js` (the house path and vertices) and a small jigsaw-edge helper, so they're exact, symmetric, and reproducible. A seeded random source is used for C's tab directions (no `Math.random()`). Colors come from the members' palettes: Becky's lake blue, Scott's ember orange, Dixie's turquoise, Detective's gold, Support's pink, and greens from Sprout. Seams are navy (#243447) lines matching the outline.

## `team/` page

Built like Star Shapes, reusing `js/ui.js`:
- "← Home". Title "Team Star". One line: "One star to represent the whole All-Stars team, for the contest due October 22. Which option should be our entry?"
- **A card per option** (lettered A–D with badges and jump links):
  - the name and a one-line description;
  - the star large;
  - an **"Where it will show"** row: email signature (about 40 px), Teams (about 32 px), inside a 96 px circle, and on a dark background;
  - **Download PNG** (1024, white square) and **Download SVG**;
  - an "Option X is my favorite" tile (one group across cards);
  - "What would you change about this option?" (500 characters).
- After the cards: "None of these yet", then "Anything else?" (1,000 characters), then **Send to Ryan** (live summary, Share, Copy, Download summary and JSON).
- Saved under `all-star-studio.team-feedback.v1` with `TEAM_VERSION` mismatch handling, the same pattern as Star Shapes.
