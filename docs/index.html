What this suggests (proposals, not decisions)

  • She leans toward pictures: no idea got "colors and patterns" alone.
  • Her preferred workflow is describe the idea → see suggested colors → adjust.
  • Main tension: she wants rich detail, but Teams avatars and email signatures show logos at roughly 32–100 px, where fine details (the kayak paddler, the dumpster's face, the phoenix's eye) disappear. The final logo needs to read at small sizes.
  • The logo brands a team. It is still open whether the entry is one team star or a set of personal stars.

Current direction (after a call with Becky, 2026-10-04)

  • Becky's picks: her overall favorite is the Outdoor "pictures and symbols" star (mountains and kayak). She also prefers the illustrated Phoenix.
  • Two tracks. (1) Team Star: one unified logo for the contest, built from "pieces of everyone" (each member's symbol contributes), due 2026-10-22. (2) Team Members: each teammate gets a personal avatar star (Becky: kayak; Scott: Phoenix; a Detective star for her boss, from the team's "detective squad" joke about research rabbit holes).
  • The only contest rule: the Aetna/CVS heart may not be used. The star should instead borrow the heart's style (straight edges, rounded corners, chunky and friendly), which is what Star Shapes explores. The chosen shape becomes the base silhouette for every later star.
  • Editing: each member page will have a free-form "what would you change?" box. Requests are sent to Ryan, and the star is redrawn and published (request and redraw). No AI in the site, no backend, no login. The site shows first names only.
  • Avatars: the app is unconfirmed, so designs are tested in a circle crop. Teams and Outlook need PNG, so PNG export is planned with Team Members.
  • House shape: B, "Softened points" (Becky's pick; F was used briefly). All star artwork uses it.
  • Team Members is live. Dixie claimed the Quick Response star. Placeholders (Support and Care, Carousel, Lighthouse, Sprout) are waiting for teammates to claim and rename them.
  • Team Star review is live (options A–D). Next: Becky picks an option, we polish it, and we prepare the final contest files before 2026-10-22.

Development at a glance

  • Static HTML, CSS, and ES-module JavaScript with native SVG. No build step, no backend, no external runtime resources.
  • Hosting is GitHub Pages from the root of the main branch. Every push to main deploys live.
  • Run locally with python3 -m http.server 8000. Run the tests with node --test 'tests/*.test.mjs'.
  • The release checklist is in CLAUDE.md: bump APP_VERSION in js/version.js, add a CHANGELOG entry, update this page (version badge, release table, status), tag vX.Y.Z, and push with tags. Tests fail if the version, the changelog, and this page disagree.
