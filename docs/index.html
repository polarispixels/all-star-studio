Prototype iteration status

Built: the Star Designs page shows four ideas (Outdoor All-Star, Support and Care, Quick Response, Phoenix Rising), each drawn two ways as original SVG stars: colors and patterns, or pictures and symbols. Each idea has a preference choice and a comment box. A short optional questionnaire with follow-ups comes after. Answers stay in the browser and can be shared, copied, or downloaded as text or JSON. Every star can be downloaded as SVG and checked at small badge size.

Not built yet: star generation, editing, prompt interpretation, PNG export, cloud feedback.

Still undecided (waiting on feedback): whether abstract, emblem, and scene modes all belong in the product; which symbols and themes deserve reusable assets first; whether art may extend outside the star; whether to start from presets, symbols, or a description; and what level of detail stays readable for her uses.
